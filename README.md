## 👋 About ArchXproject

**ArchXproject** is an independent creative & technology brand focused on building high-quality digital products, tools, and services. We combine clean design, reliable engineering, and responsive customer care to deliver an experience you can trust.

> **Our philosophy:** *Architecture in every detail. Quality in every release.*

---

## 🧩 Our Ecosystem

| Brand | Role | Description |
|:------|:-----|:------------|
| 🏗️ **ArchXproject** | Core Brand | The foundation: vision, development, and direction of everything we build. |
| 🛒 **ArchXmarket** | Store | Our official marketplace for digital products, resources, and services. |
| 🎨 **ArchXstudio** | Project Studio | Where ideas become real: design, development, and creative projects. |
| 🎧 **ArchXhotline** | Support | Fast-response channel for urgent questions and direct assistance. |
| 🛠️ **ArchXsupport** | Support | Dedicated help center for guidance, troubleshooting, and after-sales care. |

## 🚀 What We Do

- 🎯 **Product Development** – Designing and shipping polished digital products
- 🛍️ **Digital Marketplace** – Curated, reliable products through ArchXmarket
- 🎨 **Creative & Custom Projects** – Tailored work delivered by ArchXstudio
- 🤝 **Customer Care** – Real people, quick replies via Hotline & Support
